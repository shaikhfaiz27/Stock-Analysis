This Power BI project analyzes stock market investment data from 2020–2025 to monitor profit, ROI, trading activity, investor behavior, market conditions, and stock performance trends. The dashboard provides interactive insights using KPIs, charts, and slicers to help users understand market movement and investment performance.

This project helps businesses, analysts, and investment firms:

* Identify high-performing and underperforming stocks
* Compare sector-wise profitability and investment trends
* Analyze investor contribution and market participation
* Monitor risk versus return performance
* Track yearly and monthly market growth patterns
* Improve portfolio management and investment planning
* Make faster data-driven financial decisions
* Detect profitable investment opportunities
* Reduce financial risk through trend analysis
* Increase operational efficiency with centralized reporting
